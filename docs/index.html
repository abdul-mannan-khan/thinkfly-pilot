ThinkFly pilot: drone AI that thinks in proportion to need

This page gives reviewers the evidence behind the proposal's proof of concept: videos, results with intervals, how each result was scored, and what the pilot does not show yet. Code and every mission log are in the repository.

The idea in one line. The drone's AI reasons only when something happens (an event trigger). A small model on the drone proposes a plan, a rule checker tests it on the map, and only a failed check (an alarm) calls the large model at the ground station. Every model call is metered in joules.

The mission

Inspect four rooftops and come home with at least 20 % battery. Three surprises arrive as plain-language messages: a temporary no-fly zone (about 25–45 s), a sudden battery drop of 9–14 % (60–85 s) and an extra target from the operator (95–120 s). The start battery is set so the full tour only just fits. Every agent flies the same scenario. Models: Qwen3.5-4B (small) and Qwen3.5-27B (large), 4-bit, llama.cpp, JSON-schema constrained output.

How results are scored

  • Energy: net reasoning energy of every model call, summed per mission (gross minus idle power over the same window). In the HIL test the small model's energy is the Jetson module's whole input power (VDD_IN) and the large model's is measured at the ground-station GPU.
  • Safe completion: lands at home with at least the 20 % reserve, no collision, no geofence breach, no no-fly-zone violation.
  • No-fly-zone rule: a drone that is already inside a zone when it is announced gets radius ÷ 5 m/s to leave (as for an aircraft inside a newly activated restricted area). Any time inside after that, or any later entry, is a violation. The same rule applies to every agent.
  • Intervals: Clopper–Pearson 95 % for safe-completion rates; energy as median and range; energy ratios are paired by scenario.

What the pilot does not show yet

  • Free flight: the hardware test is a bench test with the propellers off (PX4 computes the physics on the board).
  • Reliability of the onboard model: on the bench, three of five small-model plans after the battery drop were faulty; the checker caught all three, but escalation cost energy. Fixing this is work package 2 of the proposal.
  • Triggers derived from control theory (the pilot's thresholds are hand-set) and a temporal-logic checker (the pilot's checker is geometric and battery-based).
  • A real battery model: the pilot uses a linear time-and-distance model.
  • Energy on the project's Jetson Orin NX and of the radio link.

Reproduce

code/test_mock.py runs the mission logic offline with mock models. The simulator runs need Isaac Sim 5.1 with Pegasus and PX4 v1.15, or AirSim 1.8.1 (AirSimNH), plus llama.cpp serving the two models; see code/thinkfly_mission.py, code/thinkfly_airsim.py and code/thinkfly_hil.py. All mission logs, with every model call's tokens, latency and energy, are in results/; this page is built from them by code/build_site.py.
